Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

Download Habit Node from the App Store, open it and follow the short setup. Pick a habit from the list or create your own, choose when you started, and check in each day.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your habits, check-ins and streaks stay on your device. We can't see them. The app does send usage statistics to Google Firebase, such as which features are used and streak numbers. For custom habits it never sends the name you typed. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Can I sync my data across devices?

No. Habit Node keeps your data on one device and doesn't sync it, so use the app on one device.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

Deleting Habit Node deletes your data. If your device is backed up to iCloud or a computer, restoring that backup brings it back.

How do I back up my data?

Habit Node's data is included in your device's normal iCloud or computer backup. Make sure backups are turned on in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → iCloud Backup.

How do I unlock Premium?

Open the upgrade screen in the app. Premium is a one-time purchase through the App Store, not a subscription. The price is shown before you buy.

I bought Premium but it isn't unlocked. What should I do?

Close and reopen the app, then tap Restore Purchases. You can check your purchase history in Settings → [your name] → Media & Purchases → View Account. If it still doesn't work, email us your App Store receipt.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy, open the upgrade screen and tap Restore Purchases.

Can I get a refund?

Apple processes all purchases, so ask Apple for refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The app crashes or doesn't work properly. What should I do?

  • Force close the app and open it again
  • Restart your device
  • Install the latest version of Habit Node
  • Update iOS

If the problem stays, email us your device model, iOS version and what happened.

How do I turn off notifications?

Change the daily reminder in the app's settings, or go to Settings → Notifications → Habit Node.

Can I suggest a feature?

Yes, please! Email your ideas to contact@tryhard-solutions.app. We can't build everything, but we read every message.

Is Habit Node a medical app?

No. Habit Node helps you track habits. Its tips are general information, not medical advice. See the Terms of Use.

Tips for getting the most out of Habit Node

Set a daily reminder

A reminder at the same time each day makes checking in automatic.

Start small

A habit you can do on a bad day lasts longer than an ambitious one.

Rate how hard it was

Rating each day shows you when the habit starts getting easier.

Add a widget

A Home Screen widget keeps your streak in view.

iPhone or iPad with iOS 18.6 or later.
